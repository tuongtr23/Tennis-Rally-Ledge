Player photos (optional)

Put one JPG per player in this folder, named after the player in lower case
with hyphens, for example:

  alexander-zverev.jpg
  jannik-sinner.jpg
  carlos-alcaraz.jpg
  alejandro-davidovich-fokina.jpg
  felix-auger-aliassime.jpg

Square images work best (they are shown in a circle). When a file is missing,
the site shows the player's initials instead.

Use only photos you have the right to publish. Official ATP and agency photos
are copyrighted.
